# Tajik old-town architectural kit

Original, procedurally generated props for the Town level (Unity URP, Android).
Every mesh and every ornament (girih star patterns, islimi scrolls, chip carving, painted ceiling,
majolica, carpet, ikat, porcelain pattern) is generated by the scripts listed below. No third-party models were used.
The only third-party inputs are CC0 Poly Haven textures. They are used as wood, plaster or bark base detail and are
resampled and recoloured into the atlases. They are listed per piece.

## Conventions (all pieces)

- Format: GLB (glTF 2.0) from the Blender 4.5.17 glTF exporter. Units are metres, +Y is up.
- **Front = glTF +Z** (Blender -Y). The level's main view direction looks at +Z faces.
- Material: PBR metallic-roughness, one texture set per piece, all JPEG embedded in the GLB:
  - base colour (sRGB; AO is already multiplied in lightly),
  - ORM (R = ambient occlusion, G = roughness, B = metallic). The same image is used as the glTF
    occlusionTexture and the metallicRoughnessTexture,
  - normal map, OpenGL convention (+G = up).
- Materials are single-sided (`doubleSided: false`). The only exception is `trellis_leaves`.
- Pivot: base centre unless noted. The hinge pivots on the door leaves are separate child nodes.
- No collision meshes, LODs or lightmap UVs are included. The UVs overlap in places (mirrored and repeated islands),
  so let Unity generate lightmap UVs if the pieces are baked.

## Generator scripts (`../scripts/`)

There is one shared library per side, and each piece has a layout module, a texture script and a build script.
Rebuild a piece by running its `*_tex.py` and then its `*_build.py`. The aivan reuses the column atlas, so run
`col_tex.py` before `aivan_tex.py`.

```
python3 -I scripts/<piece>_tex.py                 # numpy/scipy/PIL -> work/texout/<piece>_{base,orm,nrm}.jpg
/opt/blender/blender -b -P scripts/<piece>_build.py   # -> kit/<name>.glb
/opt/blender/blender -b -P scripts/render.py -- kit/<name>.glb kit/previews/<name>.png yaw=30 fit=1.1 [wall=0.5 lift=0.9 checkbf=1 ...]
```

| script | role |
|---|---|
| `texlib.py` | Ornament and texture library. It contains the Hankin polygons-in-contact girih construction on 4.8.8 and 4.6.12 tilings (8- and 12-point stars), the islimi scroll generator (sinusoidal stem, log-spiral tendrils, palmettes, leaves), chip carving, the distance-field stroke rasterizer, height → normal (OpenGL) and height → AO, and `wood_finish`, which adds grain, edge wear, recess grime, dust and bleaching. |
| `blib.py` | Blender side: an explicit mesh builder with atlas UVs (planar, developed-profile, lathe and grain-aligned `plain_fit`), the PBR material with glTF occlusion, and GLB export. |
| `door_layout.py`, `door_tex.py`, `door_build.py` | Carved double door. The panels (8-point girih, 12-point rosettes), the islimi channels on stiles, jambs and frieze, and the rope beads are relief baked into normal and AO maps. Geometry carries the panel recesses with chamfer mouldings, the stepped jamb profile, the cornice with dentils, the iron ring knockers and the studs. |
| `col_layout.py`, `col_geo.py`, `col_tex.py`, `col_build.py` | Column: stone plinth, gadrooned *kuza* base with petals, a girih band and an islimi band on the shaft, a stepped star-prism muqarnas capital with painted niche cells, the abacus and the *bolish* bracket. |
| `aivan_layout.py`, `aivan_tex.py`, `aivan_build.py` | Veranda section. Painted ceiling bays (girih in 4 colourways), barber-pole and chevron joists (*vassa*), and a girder with cartouches. All of it uses faded distemper paint with craquelure and flaking. |
| `win_layout.py`, `win_tex.py`, `win_build.py` | Panjara lattice: girih lines merged into 172 strips, each strip direction family offset 1.8 mm in depth (avoids coplanar overlap). |
| `tile_layout.py`, `tile_tex.py`, `tile_build.py` | Majolica panel. Girih field, islimi border, corner rosettes and cuerda-seca outlines, plus per-tile glaze variation, pooling, waviness, crazing, chips and grout. |
| `tand_layout.py`, `tand_tex.py`, `tand_build.py` | Tandoor. Straw-tempered clay, soot drips, heat discolouration, the air hole, and the platform with fallen plaster exposing mud brick. |
| `tap_layout.py`, `tap_tex.py`, `tap_build.py` | Tapchan. Gul carpet, adras ikat quilts and bolsters, dastarkhan cloth, a cobalt "cotton" porcelain pattern and stamped non bread. |
| `trel_layout.py`, `trel_tex.py`, `trel_build.py` | Grape trellis. Debarked poplar poles, a twisted vine, palmate low-poly leaves (cupped 9-tri fans) and grape clusters. |
| `render.py` | Preview renderer. It imports the exported GLB, so the preview shows exactly what Unity receives. `checkbf=1` paints back faces red, which is how winding and culling were checked. |

## Known weaknesses

- **Muqarnas capital**: it is a faceted star-prism stack with painted niche cells, not true muqarnas cells.
  It reads at gameplay distance, but close up it looks blocky.
- **Carving is mostly normal and AO maps.** Panel recesses, mouldings, dentils and knockers are geometry, but the girih
  and islimi are flat at grazing angles. The wood tone is a little reddish and uniform across boards.
- **Aivan atlas**: about a third of the 2048² atlas is unused. It could be packed tighter for higher ceiling
  texel density or reduced to 2048 x 1024. The column inside the aivan uses the column atlas at half density.
  There is no raised floor (*supa*).
- **Window**: tiny end-cap slivers at some V-junction tips of the lattice can show as pin-holes when viewed edge-on.
- **Tile surround**: the bricks run vertically on all four bars.
- **Trellis**: the leaves still look like flat cut-outs, canopy coverage is about 50 %, and the grape clusters are crude cones.
- **Tapchan**: the rail is plain slats, not carved panjara. The non reads a bit like a bagel from far away.
- **Door size**: each leaf is 0.618 m (1.24 m clear opening, 2.26 m high). This assumes the brief meant
  1.1–1.3 m for the whole opening. A wide courtyard gate (*darvoza*) with 1.2 m leaves would need a re-laid-out
  atlas, because changing `LEAF_W` alone overflows the 1024 layout.
- None of this has been imported into Unity yet. Phone performance has not been measured.
