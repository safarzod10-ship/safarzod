Prado sounds (stage 5). Every file is a cut / filtered / level-matched piece of a REAL recording.
Nothing is synthesised. Cutting tool: dushanbe_city_src/tools/vehicle/cut_prado_audio.py
(the raw downloads are kept in dushanbe_city_src/raw/sounds/car5/<freesound id>_*.mp3).
All Freesound sounds below are CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication, verified on each sound's page,
2026-11-05). Attribution is not required but is given.

HONEST NOTE ABOUT THE ENGINE: no freely licensed recording of a Toyota Land Cruiser Prado J150
engine was found. The idle / 1330 rpm layers, the start and the stop are a recording of a
Toyota Hilux diesel (the Hilux and the Prado 150 share the KD / 1GD-FTV 4-cylinder turbo-diesel
families; the exact engine of the recorded Hilux is not stated by the author). The 1880 rpm and
3800 rpm layers are a 1.5 l 4-cylinder diesel (Peugeot 3008 BlueHDi), i.e. a REPLACEMENT of the
same engine type (4-cylinder common-rail turbo-diesel), not a Prado.
